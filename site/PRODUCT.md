# witharosa.ai

The public site for Arosa, an AI research partner for US development
with tiered coverage. Arosa is a chat-first tool. A user names an address with its city and state,
and it reads that jurisdiction's current official zoning sources with citations, draws development
concepts in 2D and 3D, works out a unit mix, and runs a pro forma as code. New York City is deep
coverage today: the parcel resolved from NYC PLUTO, the Zoning Resolution read with citations, the
concepts set among the real neighbours with Street View and photorealistic context. Other US cities
are research coverage: the same citation discipline against their own official sources, with no
parcel adapter, computed envelope or footprint neighbours yet, and the gaps named. Every regulatory
claim carries its source; every number carries its assumptions; every answer names its jurisdiction
and coverage tier. Fourteen skills share one project state, so a change anywhere re-derives
everything downstream.

Audience: developers, owners, brokers and architects weighing what a US site could become, today
with the deepest support for New York City sites.
Visitor success: understand who Arosa is for and what it produces for a real site, trust how it treats
sources and accuracy, and apply for the private beta (`/beta`). Existing accounts sign in at
`https://build.witharosa.ai`.

Surfaces: the home page (Persuade, a pitch in seven parts: opening, proof, what it replaces, one real site, who it is for, sources, questions, then one action), the beta page, the roadmap, accuracy and help pages. The app itself is at build.witharosa.ai (app.witharosa.ai serves it too); the builder at openclaw.witharosa.ai.
