Support

Help with Arosa and the Arosa tools for ChatGPT. To see how a number is calculated, read the help articles.

Contact

Email michael@witharosa.ai. We aim to reply within two business days. To help us reproduce a problem, include your account email, the date and time, the prompt or tool you used, and a screenshot if you have one. Never send your password.

Accounts

  • Create an account: login.witharosa.ai/sign-up. Verify your email from the link we send, then sign in and approve the connection in ChatGPT.
  • Forgot your password: use "Forgot your password?" on the sign-in page. Links expire after one hour; check Spam/Junk.
  • Disconnect ChatGPT: remove the Arosa connection from your ChatGPT plugin settings. That immediately stops ChatGPT from calling the tools on your account.
  • Delete your account: email us from your account address. We delete the account and its retained evidence and confirm within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.

Common questions

  • Which places are covered? New York City today: address and BBL resolution, PLUTO parcel facts, nearby parcels and a released set of NYC and New York sources. The tools say plainly when something is outside coverage.
  • Can I rely on the answer to build? No. Arosa is a research aid with citations, not an entitlement determination. Verify against current official text and a qualified professional. See the Terms.
  • Why did a source link stop working? Retained evidence is kept for 30 days per account. Run the search again to retain a fresh copy.
  • Why was I rate-limited? Each account has hourly tool limits that protect shared upstream providers. Wait for the window to reset and try again.
  • Does it cost anything? The Arosa tools for ChatGPT are currently free to connect and use within the request limits.

How Arosa calculates it

  • How does Arosa work out how much I can build?What buildable area means, and how it is checked against filed buildings.
  • What are the three ways to build my site?As of right, most floor area and the alternative, including 485-x and the 99-unit option.
  • How is my development budget built?The lines, the default shares, and how your hard cost total is respected.
  • How does Arosa value the land?What nearby development sites sold for, how close it comes, and what a developer can pay.
  • What do Great, Good and Fair mean?The ask against what nearby sites sold for, the reasons shown, and when a listing gets no tier.
  • Where does the data come from, and how fresh is it?Every dataset, what it feeds, how often it refreshes, and where its date shows.
