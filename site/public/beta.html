A proprietary underwriting engine

Arosa runs on frontier AI models, directed by an engine purpose-built for New York development underwriting. Every site gets the same disciplined math, at your own assumptions.

  1. Floor area

    Computes buildable floor area from current zoning, including affordability bonuses and wide-street rules.

  2. Exits

    Prices rental and condo exits.

  3. Taxes

    Carries full property taxes and the 485-x exemption, including the 99-unit option.

  4. Land value

    Computes residual land value at your own hard costs, soft costs, financing and target returns.

  5. Deal tier

    Rates each deal great, good or fair, and gives the reason.

  6. Data checks

    Flags data problems alongside the result.

A research agent that works the site end to end

Name an address. The agent works the site in order, then writes up what it found.

  1. Confirm the lot

    Matches the address to its tax lot and parcel record.

  2. Size the building

    Reads current zoning and computes what can be built.

  3. Price it

    Prices the exits, with property taxes and 485-x.

  4. Test the land price

    Sets residual land value against the asking price.
