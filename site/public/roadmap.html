What Arosa does today, and what is next.

See what is available today and what is coming next for property research, Underwriting and architectural Modeling.

In Arosa today

01
Site intake

An address with its city and state becomes an identified site in its jurisdiction. In New York City, an address, a building name or a BBL becomes a confirmed parcel with its boundary, the neighbors from NYC building footprints, and nearby transit, parks and retail.

02
Zoning engine

The site's own zoning code, read live and cited by section in any city. In New York City, also districts, overlays and special districts from the parcel record, FAR by use against the existing building, and curated rules with their Zoning Resolution sections, read as starting points to verify.

03
Entitlement

The mechanisms that could add floor area or change use, with the approvals and recorded instruments each one needs.

04
Massing study

Concepts drawn in 2D and 3D with computed areas, FAR and height, compared side by side in any city. In New York City they are also placed among the footprint neighbors, with Street View and photorealistic context, and rendered from the street and from above.

05
Program and unit mix

Market inputs recorded with their sources, and a unit mix by bedroom type sized to the residential area and the efficiency you expect.

07
Floor plans

A typical-floor plan for each option, with rooms, walls, doors and windows you can edit, and a check that every room is closed.

09
Financial model

A line-item development budget, revenue, NOI, yield on cost, value, returns and sensitivities, run as code from the inputs you supply, with the arithmetic shown.

13
Final deliverable

A seven-slide underwriting deck drawn from the site, to print or save as a PDF.

Next

06
Parking optimization

Required and achievable parking, layouts and ramps, under each jurisdiction's own rules; the Manhattan Core rules are encoded today.

08
Efficiency optimization

Net-to-gross, unit counts and layout choices tuned against each other.

10
Feasibility recommendation

The option worth pursuing, with the evidence and the risks laid out.

11
Architectural concepting

Facade and material directions that fit the site and the program.

12
Iterative design

Version history for concepts; revise, branch and compare.

14
Independent workspaces

Start Modeling or Underwriting with a property address. Develop a design or evaluate a deal independently, with an optional transfer of selected quantities.

15
Editable IFC import

Bring an existing building into Modeling as editable walls, levels, doors and windows. Review unsupported elements and retain the original model.

16
Scan validation

Compare an aligned point cloud with model surfaces, inspect discrepancy maps and review distances at a chosen tolerance.

17
Assisted building reconstruction

Turn scans into reviewable existing-condition models, then develop proposed designs as separate revisions.
