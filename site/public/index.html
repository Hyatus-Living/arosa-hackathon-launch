Where the numbers come from.

The city's own records and dated listing feeds. Every regulatory claim is cited to its section and every figure to its source, with the date it was read, so you can defend it to partners and lenders.

How each source is used and how fresh it is

  • PLUTOTax-lot data: lot size, frontage, existing building, districts and landmark flags.
  • Zoning ResolutionThe current text, including the December 2024 City of Yes changes, read section by section.
  • DOB filingsDepartment of Buildings records for the existing building and for new buildings near each site, three years back.
  • ACRISRecorded sales, deeds and air-rights instruments, used as comps.
  • HPD registrationsHousing Preservation and Development registrations and violations.
  • ListingsFor-sale listings from major listing platforms and brokerage feeds, refreshed every six hours.

Questions, answered.

What does Arosa do?
Name a New York City lot or listing. Arosa confirms the tax lot, computes what current zoning lets you build, prices three ways to build it as rentals or condos, and works out what the land is worth at your own costs.
Where does the data come from?
The city's own records: PLUTO lot data, the Zoning Resolution including the December 2024 City of Yes text, DOB filings, ACRIS records and HPD registrations. Listings come from major listing platforms and brokerage feeds, refreshed every six hours.
How current is it?
Listings refresh every six hours. Each source shows the date it was read, so you can see how fresh every figure is.
How accurate is it?
Every regulatory claim is cited to its section and every figure to its source. Buildable floor area has been checked against the city's filed new buildings; the results are here. Where five or more development sites nearby have sold, land value starts from what they sold for; on sites set aside to test it, that figure was within half to double the price paid 89% of the time. What a developer can pay at a target return carries no accuracy claim.
Can I use my own costs and returns?
Yes. Hard costs, soft costs, financing and target returns are yours. Change one and every option, budget and land value updates.
Does it replace my architect or land-use attorney?
No. Arosa is a first pass that tells you which sites are worth their time. It is a research aid, not an entitlement determination.
What does it cost?
Use is unlimited during the private beta. Beta firms get 50% off their first year after launch.
Does it work outside New York City?
New York City is covered lot by lot. Elsewhere, Arosa researches that city's own zoning code with citations and says plainly what it cannot compute.
