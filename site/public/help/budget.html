Help/The budget

How is my development budget built?

The budget splits the total cost of a building into the lines a developer expects to see. The lines add up exactly to the total cost Arosa uses to value the land.

The four groups

GroupWhat is in itHow it is set
Land and acquisitionLand value, or your purchase price when you give one. Transfer taxes. Closing costs.Buyer transfer tax applies to one- to three-family sales, at the rate for the price. Closing costs are 1.5% of the price.
Hard costsNine lines, from site work to contingency.Your hard cost per gross square foot times gross building area. The default is $375, set separately for rentals and condos. Mid-rise (7 to 12 storeys) costs 1.12 times that, high-rise 1.25 times, and lots under 25 feet wide 1.08 times.
Soft costsSix lines, plus the 485-x construction wage floor on rentals of 100 units or more.A share of hard cost. The default is 22%.
Financing and carryInterest and fees while the building is built.A share of land, taxes, hard and soft cost. The default is 10%.

The defaults are Arosa planning figures dated October 15, 2026, not a cost index. The date shows beside each assumption in the listing detail.

How the lines are split

Each group is divided by shares that add up to 100%. A walk-up of five storeys or fewer has no elevator, and that share goes to the other hard cost lines in proportion.

Shares are rounded planning figures last set on October 21, 2026, not a contractor estimate.

How your own costs change it

Your hard cost per square foot sets the hard cost total. Budget shares only divide that total: change a share and dollars move between lines, never the total. Change your hard cost, soft cost or carry and every option and the land value are priced again, with the lines still adding up.

What it does not cover

It has no line for environmental cleanup, tenant relocation or buyouts, lease-up reserves, or conditions specific to one site’s soil or building.
