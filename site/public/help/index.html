Why does Arosa show this?

Why does a listing have no deal tier?

A tier needs an asking price, a match to one tax lot, residential use as of right, and either five or more development site sales nearby or a building that pays something for the land at Fair returns. Without those the listing shows Unpriced or no tier, with the reason. How tiers work.

Why can a developer pay nothing for the land?

At the rents, costs and returns used, the building is worth less than it costs to build before any land price. It is most common where rents are low. It is not a statement that the land is worthless: where five or more development sites nearby have sold, the land value is what they sold for. How land value works.

Why does a result differ from ZoLa?

ZoLa shows the district and its maximum floor area ratio on paper. Arosa also fits a building on the lot, using its shape, yards, height and setbacks, and takes the lower figure. It reads the current Zoning Resolution, including City of Yes, where PLUTO may lag. Neither is an entitlement determination. How buildable area works.
