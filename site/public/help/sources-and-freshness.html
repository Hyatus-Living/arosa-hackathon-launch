Where does Arosa’s data come from, and how fresh is it?

Every figure comes from a named public record, a listing feed, or an Arosa planning assumption with a date. The date shown is when Arosa last read the source, not when the city last changed the record.

What it does not cover

Arosa reads public records and listing feeds. It does not see off-market sales, private rent rolls, or anything filed with the city after the date shown.

Common questions

Does Arosa use asking prices as sale prices?

No. Sale prices per square foot come from recorded closings only. When a ZIP has too few closings, Arosa says so and uses a dated planning figure instead.
