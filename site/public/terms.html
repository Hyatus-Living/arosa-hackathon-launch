The service

Arosa provides property and zoning research tools, including the Arosa tools for ChatGPT. The tools resolve properties in US cities, report public parcel data where available (such as NYC Planning PLUTO attributes in New York City), list nearby parcel candidates, and search and fetch retained source evidence with citations. Coverage depth varies by city and may change over time.

Research aid, not professional advice

Arosa supports source-backed research; it does not make determinations. Nothing in Arosa is legal, architectural, engineering, tax or investment advice, and no output establishes zoning compliance, available development rights, a buildable envelope or any entitlement. Parcel attributes such as floor-area ratio and lot area do not prove what may legally be built. Always check the current zoning text, overlays, landmarks status and agency approvals, and consult qualified professionals before relying on any result.
