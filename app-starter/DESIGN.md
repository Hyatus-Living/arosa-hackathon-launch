# Arosa research workspace

The app carries the witharosa.ai brand contract verbatim: a pure-monochrome OKLCH ramp (neutrals at chroma 0), the stepped-parcel glyph as the only logo, and Geist with Geist Mono for figures. The day view is the default; the header toggle switches to the night view and the saved choice wins. Color is reserved for state — success, warning, danger, info — and lives in the answer, never the frame. The conversation owns the page.

A main menu rail runs down the left of every page: Home, Saved sites and What's new, then a site's own Property and Sources while a site is open, then the recent conversations ending in All conversations, which opens History in the chat drawer, then Teams, Wallet, Admin, Appearance and the account with sign out. On wide screens it collapses to its icons and remembers that choice; on narrow screens it is a modal drawer opened from the top bar. Each page has a slim top bar that names it (a site and its floor plan show a breadcrumb back up) and holds that page's actions.

Home is the map and the top deals: the ranked listings beside a full-height map and recent sites under the list. One place search names the location: a borough, ZIPs, a neighborhood and a zoning prefix become removable tokens, and an address or BBL is underwritten. Under it, one row holds the four quick filters, an All filters button that opens every filter in a right-hand drawer (a bottom sheet on phones), and the cost assumptions apart from the filters. Sort and sharing sit with the result count, and map-following is a toggle on the map. The chat is one right-hand drawer on every page, opened from the ask bar and closed from its header, and remembered per viewer. Chat history lives in the rail, and History, the conversation search, takes the thread's place inside the drawer; there is no separate chat page, and an old /chat link opens home with the drawer open. Without listings to show, home is the address entry and recent sites with the drawer open beside them on wide screens; once listings arrive or the viewer moves to another page, their own choice for the drawer returns. A conversation opened from a shared or saved link, or restored on home, opens the drawer at any width, so its answer is always in view. An example question starts its own conversation in the drawer, never one already open there. Pages keep their last row clear of the ask bar. Signed out, the drawer stays closed and home's welcome carries the notice to sign in again.

Filter panels use the browser top layer, stay within the viewport and scroll when a short window requires it. Filters apply as they change, in the row and in the drawer alike, so the map and the count follow while the drawer is open. The map and result controls remain available when the chat closes. At narrow widths, list and map are alternate views, the chat drawer opens over the page until closed, and the recent conversations sit in the menu drawer. Compact listing thumbnails keep the results readable beside chat. Property details and sources open in a stable overlay drawer; opening one must not resize streamed text.

Messages have a restrained user bubble and unboxed assistant prose. Citations are compact inline controls; a source summary expands into readable references. Research activity is collapsed by default, can expand inline and collapses after completion. Interactive generated content remains isolated, but ordinary answers and tools are native document content.

Motion communicates state: one short entrance for a newly submitted message, stable incremental answer text, a quiet three-dot working indicator, a brief drawer transition and predictable composer growth. Never replay entrance motion on tokens. Honor reduced motion. Do not automatically scroll a user who is reading earlier content; provide a return-to-latest control.

Implementation uses semantic HTML, shared CSS tokens and focused rendering modules. Image studies inform composition and interaction states; the interface remains native, accessible document content.
