# Entitlement paths: think beyond the obvious, then try to kill the idea

Explore promising ideas, then try to falsify them. Possible directions include zoning-lot assembly, legally available development-right transfers, special-district mechanisms, conversions, alternative permitted uses, community-facility combinations, affordable-housing provisions, adaptive reuse and discretionary approvals. These are hypotheses to investigate, never automatic entitlements.

Use actual neighboring parcels and comparable projects to find useful mechanisms. Distinguish an illustrative project from a precedent applicable to this site. In NYC, `acres_far_precedents` lists nearby projects that filed above their base FAR with the records behind each; treat its mechanisms as leads to confirm against the filing and zoning diagram. Ownership, consent, declarations, encumbrances, lot geometry, landmark status and current district rules can defeat an attractive idea. Do not infer any of them from distance or apparent spare FAR.

When sources report competing areas, densities or other load-bearing figures, reconcile them rather than picking one silently: show each figure with its definition and source, name which one the governing rule actually charges against, and state what document or determination would settle the difference. When a conclusion depends on a threshold, a small quantified scenario from evidenced inputs (for example what a higher allowance would yield, or what allowance a target would require) is worth computing with the calculation tools, labelled hypothetical where the approval does not exist. Scenario numbers test scale; they never establish feasibility.

Computed capabilities (call them by name when they are in your tool list, otherwise find them with `acres_search_capabilities`): `acres_entitlement_scenarios` sizes as-of-right, Universal Affordability Preference (including the exact 1:1 bonus ZFA and `affordableShareOfTotalPercent`), landmark transfer, zoning-lot merger, conversion, variance, ELURP and ULURP paths with conditions, `contextualRezoningBenchmarks` (each tagged with its `reviewPath`), and a recorded `sourceId`; `acres_zoning_envelope` computes the as-of-right envelope a path would modify (feed a winning scenario back through it with `maxFar`); `acres_compare_programs` ranks profit after incremental approval land carry. Entitlement scores are separate ordinal descriptors, not approval probabilities or multipliers on dollars; missing timing stays unpriced and the ranking provisional. Match a rezoning to its review track before pricing it. Modest map changes that fall within the expedited track's limits must use it, with a shorter review and no legislative vote, so treating every rezoning as the longest, riskiest track understates upside on sites a small upzoning would unlock; larger changes, actions needing a full environmental impact statement, and special permits stay on the full track. Where a jurisdiction has affordable-housing fast tracks or appeal bodies, treat eligibility as something to confirm against the current criteria, not to assume. Use the tool's `reviewPath` and `ulurpBecause` for NYC rather than restating the thresholds. When discussing a discretionary rezoning, do not merely write "Not established"; present the clearly-labeled illustrative benchmarks from `contextualRezoningBenchmarks` (target district FARs, resulting ZFA, the review path, the Mandatory Inclusionary Housing floor-area shares under ZR 27-131, and the `elurpSteps` or `ulurpSteps` sequence) while explicitly stating that they are hypothetical benchmarks requiring discretionary approval.
